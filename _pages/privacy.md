This privacy policy covers **idiomvideos4kids**, a personal automation tool run by the
owner of this website to publish videos to the owner's own YouTube channels.

## What the app accesses
When the channel owner signs in with Google, the app requests these YouTube permissions:

- **Upload and manage videos** (`youtube.upload`, `youtube.force-ssl`): to upload videos,
  thumbnails and captions to the owner's own channels.
- **View YouTube Analytics reports** (`yt-analytics.readonly`): to read view statistics for
  the owner's own channels for internal weekly reports.

## How data is used and stored
- Access tokens are stored only on the owner's private computer and are used only to
  perform the actions above.
- No Google user data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
- The app is not offered to the public; it is used only with accounts the owner controls.

## Revoking access
Access can be removed at any time at
[myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).

## Contact
Questions about this policy: open an issue at
[github.com/kyoangel/kyoangel.github.io](https://github.com/kyoangel/kyoangel.github.io/issues).
